HA Click-to-Call

Dial a number on your phone from your browser.

1Add the blueprint to Home Assistant

2Create the automation

  1. Select HA Click-to-Call in the blueprint list.
  2. Android phone: select your phone.
  3. Screen-on sensor (optional): select your phone's Interactive sensor to clear the notification once you see the dialer.
  4. Webhook ID: paste this:
  5. Select Save.

3Connect the extension

Chrome will ask permission for this extension to "read and change your data" on this server. This permission means the extension can send the phone number from your browser to your Home Assistant server.

4Set up your phone

Allow the Home Assistant app to Display over other apps. Android needs this before an app can open the dialer for you. Without it, your phone still buzzes, and you tap the notification to open the dialer.

On your phone, go to Settings > Apps > Home Assistant, then turn on Display over other apps (called Appear on top on some phones). The Home Assistant app may also open this screen for you the first time a number arrives.
